CutTrack AI privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

CutTrack AI is a personal calorie and macro tracker built and used by Avijeet Gupta. This page explains what the app does with your data.

What is stored, and where

  • Your food log, targets, weight entries and saved foods are stored on your phone and in your own Google Drive, in a folder called CutTrack AI.
  • The app uses Google's drive.file permission. It can only see and change files it created itself. It cannot read anything else in your Drive.
  • There is no separate database. The app's server keeps no copy of your log.

Google account data

When you sign in, the app receives your email address, used only to confirm it is the allowed account, and permission to manage its own Drive files. It does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google data.

AI processing

When you use AI features, such as typing an unusual food, sending a photo or voice note, or asking the coach, that text, image or audio is sent to an AI provider (OpenAI, Google Gemini or Groq) to get an answer. Photos and audio are not saved by CutTrack. Each provider handles the data under its own API terms.

Deleting your data

  • Delete entries in the app, or delete the CutTrack AI folder in Google Drive.
  • Remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
